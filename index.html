Click the game once so the keyboard works. Pad: stick/D-pad move (up jump, down crouch, back = block), X/Y/B = Light/Medium/Heavy, Start pause, View reset, L3 hitboxes, R3 dummy mode.

Keyboard P1: WASD + F/G/H. Esc pause, Backspace reset, V hitboxes, N dummy mode. Chain L > M > H on hit or block. Powers: A (T) = element fireball, down+A = rising uppercut, forward+A = rush strike, back+A = phase step (teleports behind the enemy), hold Heavy + A = triple shot, A in the air = meteor dive, RT (R) = super beam at 50% meter. LB (E) swaps your element: Fire burns (fire on wet = steam), Water soaks and slows and puts out fire, Air = fast shots and huge knockback, Earth = extra damage and stun. Specials cancel out of normals on hit. R3 / N cycles BOT and dummy modes.
